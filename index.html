About Mannabay Foundation

The Mannabay Foundation is a Kenyan nonprofit based in Nakuru, Rift Valley. Founded in 2016, we focus on uplifting youth — especially boys and young men — by providing support in education, mental health, economic empowerment, and leadership skills.

Our Mission

To stand up for the forgotten boy child and young people by fostering self‑awareness, stability and opportunities that build confidence, resilience, and purpose.
